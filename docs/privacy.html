Privacy

What Air Sound does with your data.

Last updated October 29, 2026.

Air Sound is a pair of native apps that send audio from your Mac to your iPhone over your local network. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no cloud service.

Audio

The Mac app captures system audio only while you have sharing turned on. The audio is held in memory, sent directly to the iPhone, and played there. It is never written to disk and never sent to a server. The Mac app excludes its own output from the capture.

Network

The Mac advertises itself on the local network with Bonjour. The iPhone connects directly to the Mac over TCP. Audio does not pass through Air Sound servers because there are none. Both devices need to reach each other on the same network.

Permissions

  • System Audio Recording (macOS): used to capture the audio that plays on the Mac.
  • Local Network (iOS): used to find the Mac and open the connection.

No microphone, camera, contacts, location, or other data is requested.

Data collection

Air Sound does not collect personal data. There is no telemetry, crash reporting, or usage tracking in either app. The apps do not create an account or ask for an email address.

This website

This site is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. GitHub may process standard web request data, such as IP addresses, to serve the page. GitHub's own privacy terms apply to that hosting.

Support

If you open an issue on GitHub, the information you choose to include is visible there. It is used to answer the issue and for nothing else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
